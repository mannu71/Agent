How the desk decides
  1. Every hour the newest 1-minute Binance prices arrive. They are combined into 1-hour and 4-hour candles; a candle is used only after it closes.
  2. Each concept is an exact rule (sweep of a swing level, fair value gap, order block, change of character, higher low, Wyckoff spring, 80% rule). A match becomes a setup with an entry, a stop beyond the pattern and a target at 2R or the edge of the range.
  3. Every setup is played out minute by minute with Delta Exchange fees, slippage and funding. Its result goes into the memory, traded or not.
  4. The agent takes a setup only if at least 30 past setups in the same situation (concept, timeframe, direction, daily trend) averaged +0.05R or better, with the average reliably above zero. Until then it says no trade.
  5. Rules were frozen on 7 Oct 2026; everything from 1 Oct is unseen data. The full record is a tamper-evident journal in the repository.
