# Related rule-based strategy papers (Zarattini / Concretum family and others precise enough to code)

Research note scope: exact rules, reported results, and replication status for the Concretum Group / Carlo Zarattini paper family (with Aziz, Barbon, Pagani, Wilcox, Antonacci, Stamatoudis), plus Kakushadze & Serur "151 Trading Strategies" as a contrast. Compiled 2026-10-05.

**Access caveat (important for the report writer):** SSRN, concretumgroup.com, alexandria.unisg.ch (St. Gallen PDFs), cdn.prod.website-files.com, arxiv, quantpedia, quantitativo, mql5, and substack were all blocked by the network proxy, so **no primary PDF was read directly**. Rules below come from (a) search-engine extracts of the abstracts and author pages, (b) independent replication repos on GitHub that quote the papers' rules and tables (cloned and read in full), and (c) secondary write-ups. Each item is tagged with its source. Anything not confirmed by at least one source quoting the paper is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Q1. Exact rules, parameters and reported results for each paper

### Takeaway
The four core Concretum strategies can be coded from public sources: (1) the 5-min ORB on QQQ, (2) the 5-min ORB on the "Stocks in Play" top-20 relative-volume names, (3) the SPY "Noise Area" intraday momentum strategy with a VWAP trailing stop and 2% vol targeting, and (4) the 9-lookback Donchian ensemble for crypto. All four share the same parameter habits: 14-day lookbacks, 1% risk per trade or a volatility target, a 4x intraday leverage cap (200% for daily strategies), and costs that cover commission but little or no slippage. The two daily trend papers (ATH stocks, industry trends) are also fully specified. The gap/price-action paper is not a mechanical system.

### Cited Findings

#### A. "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" (Zarattini & Aziz, SSRN 4416622, April 2023): QQQ 5-min ORB
- Sample: QQQ 5-minute bars, Jan 1 2016 to Feb 17 2023, $25,000 starting capital — [CXO / danfin search extract](https://danfin.net/opening-range-breakout-research); [replication README](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Direction rule: the first 5-min bar (09:30–09:35 ET) sets the direction. Bullish bar (close > open) means long only; bearish bar means short only; doji means no trade — [giovannibrusco replication](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Entry: at the **open of the second 5-min bar (09:35)** in the direction of the first bar. This is not a stop-order breakout, which differs from the Stocks-in-Play paper — [giovannibrusco replication](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq); [danfin](https://danfin.net/opening-range-breakout-research)
- Stop: the opposite extreme of the first 5-min bar (long: low of the 09:30 bar; short: high). That distance defines 1R — [giovannibrusco](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Target: +10R, otherwise flat at the session close. The +10R target is hit on only ~2–3% of trades; ~75% of trades exit on the stop and ~22% at the close — [giovannibrusco](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Sizing: shares = min(1% equity / R, 4 × equity / entry price), i.e. 1% risk with a 4x FINRA day-trading leverage cap — [giovannibrusco](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Costs: commission of **$0.0005/share per side**; **no slippage** ("we assumed no slippage in fills") — [giovannibrusco FIGURE_MAP / README](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- Reported results: 1,484% total return vs 169% for QQQ buy-and-hold (2016–2023); annualized alpha 33% net of commissions — [Concretum / SSRN search extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4416622). Paper figures quoted by the replicator: **1,795 trades, Sharpe 1.12**; secondary summary: 24% hit rate, +0.13R per trade, ~33%/yr with 4x leverage — [giovannibrusco](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq); [danfin](https://danfin.net/opening-range-breakout-research)
- The paper also discusses using TQQQ (3x ETF) to get leverage within constraints — [SSRN abstract extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4416622)
- No out-of-sample period in the paper — [danfin](https://danfin.net/opening-range-breakout-research)

#### B. "A Profitable Day Trading Strategy For The U.S. Equity Market" (Zarattini, Barbon, Aziz, SSRN 4729284, Feb 16 2024; SFI RP 24-98): 5-min ORB on Stocks in Play
- Universe: more than 7,000 US stocks, 2016–2023 — [search extract of SSRN/Quantified Strategies](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/stocks-in-play-trading-strategy-day-trading/)
- Eligibility filters (computed on prior days): open price > $5; 14-day average volume ≥ 1,000,000 shares; 14-day ATR > $0.50 — [CXO extract](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/day-trading-with-an-opening-range-breakout-strategy/); [POB-CL-DT PR #12 code](https://github.com/patrickobirmingham-eng/POB-CL-DT/pull/12)
- Stocks in Play selection: relative volume = volume of today's first 5-minute bar ÷ average first-5-minute volume over the previous 14 days. Keep RV ≥ 1 (100%) and trade the **top 20 by RV** each day — [POB-CL-DT PR #12](https://github.com/patrickobirmingham-eng/POB-CL-DT/pull/12); [Hummingbird implementation notes (secondary)](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md)
- Direction: a bullish first 5-min bar allows only a long on a break of the bar's high; a bearish bar allows only a short on a break of its low; a doji is skipped — [Quantified Strategies extract](https://substack.com/@quantifiedstrategies/note/c-159355254)
- Entry: stop order at the 5-min high (long) or low (short), any time after 09:35 — [POB-CL-DT PR #12](https://github.com/patrickobirmingham-eng/POB-CL-DT/pull/12)
- Stop: **10% of the 14-day ATR** from entry. Paper example: BLDR with ATR $5 gives a $0.50 stop; short at $174.44, stop at $174.94 — [Hummingbird (secondary, quotes paper example)](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md); [POB-CL-DT](https://github.com/patrickobirmingham-eng/POB-CL-DT/pull/12)
- Exit: end of day; no profit target, no trailing stop — [POB-CL-DT](https://github.com/patrickobirmingham-eng/POB-CL-DT/pull/12)
- Sizing: 1% risk per trade with a 4x notional (leverage) cap — [search extract](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/day-trading-with-an-opening-range-breakout-strategy/). **AMBIGUOUS:** whether 1% is of total equity per trade or 1% of a 1/20 "slot". The POB-CL-DT replication uses slots (equity/20, 1% of slot, 4x slot notional cap); the Hummingbird summary uses 1% of the whole account with a 4x total-portfolio cap. Not resolvable without the PDF.
- Costs: commission **$0.0035/share** (IB Pro). Performance is stated net of commissions — [Hummingbird (secondary)](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md). Whether slippage was modelled is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Headline results (top-20 Stocks in Play, 5-min ORB, 2016–2023): total net return **>1,600% (1,637%)**, **Sharpe 2.81**, **annualized alpha 36% (35.8%)**, beta ≈ 0; S&P 500 returned 198% over the same window — [SSRN abstract extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284); [Hummingbird](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md)
- Additional figures quoted by a secondary source (**treat as UNVERIFIED** until checked against the PDF): IRR 41.6%/yr; max drawdown 12%; worst day −1.61%; "hit ratio" 48.4%, possibly a daily hit ratio, since per-stock win rates cited are 10–24%; trading all filtered stocks without the RV filter gave only ~3.2%/yr. ORB window comparison: 5-min 1,637% / Sharpe 2.81; 15-min 272% / 1.43; 30-min 21% / 0.21; 60-min 39% / 0.40. Average PnL by RV bucket: RV < 100% gives −0.02R; RV > 100% gives +0.08R; RV > 3000% gives +0.38R — [Hummingbird-Project notes](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md)

#### C. "Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY)" (Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon, SSRN 4824172, May 2024; SFI RP 24-97)
- Noise area: for each minute-of-day t, σ_t = mean over the last **14 trading days** of |P_{d,t}/Open_d − 1| (the average absolute move from the open at that time of day) — [replication SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [Concretum substack extract](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/beat-the-market-with-intraday-momentum)
- Bands with gap adjustment: UpperBound_t = **max(Open_today, Close_yesterday)** × (1 + σ_t); LowerBound_t = **min(Open_today, Close_yesterday)** × (1 − σ_t). The replicator notes that many replications get the gap anchoring wrong — [replication SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [CXO extract](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- Entry: signals are checked only at HH:00 and HH:30 (10:00, 10:30, … 15:30 ET). Price > upper band means long; price < lower band means short. Flips are allowed — [SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- Exits: "base" exit is the opposite band. The paper's preferred "final" exit is a trailing stop at **max(UpperBound, session VWAP)** for longs and **min(LowerBound, VWAP)** for shorts, checked at the same 30-minute marks. All positions close at 16:00 (no overnight risk) — [SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- Sizing: volatility target of **2% daily** using SPY's realized daily volatility over the past **14 days**, with leverage capped at **4x**. Exposure = min(Equity × 0.02/σ_d, 4 × Equity) — [RePEc/search extract](https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2497.html); [replication sizing.py](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Costs: IB commission **$0.0035/share** (min $0.35). The paper also discusses slippage, but its exact slippage value is **UNVERIFIED** — [replication SPEC/backtest.py](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [search extract](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- Results (May 2007 to early 2024, net): total return **1,985%**, annualized **19.6%**, **Sharpe 1.33**, alpha ~19.6%, beta ≈ 0 — [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2497.html); [SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Exit ablation (CXO summary): the basic version gives 6.2%/yr and Sharpe 0.61; the current-band + VWAP stop gives 9.7%/yr and Sharpe 1.24 (presumably before vol-targeted sizing; **UNVERIFIED** context) — [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/complex-intraday-time-series-momentum-strategy-applied-to-spy/)
- The paper reports Sharpe rising with VIX, to ~3.5 when VIX > 40 (as cited in the replication spec) — [SPEC](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Max drawdown, hit ratio and trade count from the paper itself: **not found** (see Gaps).
- The paper took 4th place in the Quantpedia Awards 2025 — [FSI St. Gallen news](https://fsi.unisg.ch/de/newsdetail/news/quantpedia-awards-2025-fsis-barbon-and-team-secure-4th-place-with-intraday-momentum-strategy/)

#### E. "Catching Crypto Trends: A Tactical Approach for Bitcoin and Altcoins" (Zarattini, Pagani, Barbon, SSRN 5209907, April 2025; SFI RP)
All items below come from a detailed review of the 38-page PDF in [alfred1123/Quant_Strategies PR #61](https://github.com/alfred1123/Quant_Strategies/pull/61) (file `docs/research/catching-crypto-trends.md`), which labels each item as [PAPER] or [UNSTATED].
- Data: CoinMarketCap daily OHLCV aggregated across exchanges, Jan 2010 to Mar 2025, survivorship-bias-free. BTC results run Jan 1 2015 to Mar 19 2025. Signals use the **close**.
- Lookbacks n ∈ {5, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 150, 250, 360}, equal-weighted (N = 9).
- DonchianUp_n = max of the last n closes, *including today*; DonchianDown_n = min; Mid = (Up + Down)/2.
- Position: Pos_n = 1 if Close == Up_n (a new n-day closing high); Pos_n = 0 if Close ≤ TrailingStop_n; otherwise unchanged. Long/flat only.
- Trailing stop: TrailingStop_n(t+1) = max(TrailingStop_n(t), Mid_n(t)), so it only ratchets up. On entry it is initialized at the mid-line.
- Sizing: w_n = min(**0.25/σ_t, 2.00**) × Pos_n, where σ_t is the asset's **90-day annualized volatility**. Target vol is 25% and the allocation cap is 200%. Combo weight = average of the 9 w_n. The weight is set at the close of t and earns the t+1 return.
- Rebalancing: trade only when |current − target| > **20%**, and only for vol-driven changes. Breakouts and stop exits trade immediately. Net results charge **10 bps** (sensitivity tested at 0/10/25/50 bps).
- BTC trade counts, gross, 2015–2025: 5d 292, 10d 156, 20d 78, 30d 49, 60d 28, 90d 20, 150d 15, 250d 9, 360d 5.
- BTC combo, gross: CAGR 30%, vol 17%, Sharpe 1.58, max DD 19%, alpha 14%, beta 0.17.
- Net of 10 bps plus the 20% threshold: BTC CAGR 30%, Sharpe 1.56, max DD 19%; ETH 27% / 1.51 / 15%; BNB 17% / 1.06 / 17%; SOL 27% / 1.68 / 12%. Buy-and-hold BTC drawdown was >80%.
- Rotational top-20 portfolio: universe ranked by 30-day median dollar volume, equal capital, monthly reconstitution. Eligibility: ≥365 days listed and 30-day median daily volume ≥ $2M. Removal: median volume < $1M or 30-day median absolute daily price change < 0.5%. Net results: CAGR 18%, vol 9%, Sharpe 1.57, Sortino 1.97, max DD 11%, alpha 10.8% vs BTC, beta 0.08. Headline figures also confirmed by [SSRN abstract extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5209907) and [Concretum substack](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/catching-crypto-trends).
- Internal inconsistencies flagged by the reviewer: the combo MAR is printed as 0.88 while 30/19 ≈ 1.58; Sortino drops from 2.03 gross to 1.23 net while Sharpe barely moves; the 5-day gross CAGR is quoted as both 34% and 36% — [PR #61](https://github.com/alfred1123/Quant_Strategies/pull/61)
- [UNSTATED] in the paper: return type and annualization for σ (√365 vs √252), same-bar entry/exit precedence, whether the 20% band is relative or absolute, the base on which the 10 bps is charged, slippage, and the daily close time (UTC cut-off) — [PR #61](https://github.com/alfred1123/Quant_Strategies/pull/61)

#### G. "Does Trend-Following Still Work on Stocks?" (Zarattini, Pagani, Wilcox, SSRN 5084316, Jan 2025)
- Revisits Wilcox & Crittenden (2005, data 1980–2004). New data: survivorship-bias-free liquid US stocks, 1950 to Nov 2024 — [SSRN/CXO extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5084316)
- Liquidity filter: unadjusted close > **$10** and 42-trading-day average dollar volume > **$1M** — [search extract](https://concretumgroup.com/does-trend-following-still-work-on-stocks/)
- Entry: the stock makes a new **all-time high** (long-only) — [search extract](https://concretumgroup.com/does-trend-following-still-work-on-stocks/)
- Exit: trailing stop at **10 × the 42-day ATR** below price (dynamically adjusted) — [search extract](https://harbourfrontquant.substack.com/p/does-trend-following-still-work-on)
- Sizing: inverse-volatility so each holding contributes equal volatility; **leverage capped at 200%** — [search extract](https://harbourfrontquant.substack.com/p/does-trend-following-still-work-on). The exact vol-target number is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Results: more than 66,000 trades, with fewer than 7% of trades driving cumulative profit. Core statistics hold out-of-sample (2005–2024), with a modest decline in average trade after the original publication. Theoretical portfolio 1991–2024, **gross**: CAGR **15.19%**, alpha **6.18%**. Daily turnover is a major cost problem; a "Turnover Control" algorithm makes it viable after fees, and portfolios < $1M are hurt most without it — [SSRN/CXO extract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5084316); [search extract](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/trend-following-strategy-for-stocks/)
- **UNVERIFIED / not found:** net-of-cost CAGR, Sharpe, max DD, win rate, and the turnover-control rule itself.

#### I. Other Zarattini papers (2023–2026) found

#### J. Contrast: Kakushadze & Serur, "151 Trading Strategies" (SSRN 3247865; Palgrave 2018)
- A catalogue of 150+ strategies with 550+ formulas across 20 chapters: options, stocks, ETFs, fixed income, indexes, volatility, FX, commodities, futures, structured assets, convertibles, tax arbitrage, misc, distressed, real estate, cash, cryptocurrencies, global macro, infrastructure. Includes some ML strategies (ANN, Bayes, kNN) and source code for out-of-sample backtesting — [SSRN abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3247865); [Palgrave](https://www.palgrave.com/978-3-030-02791-9)
- It gives formal signal definitions (e.g. momentum, mean-reversion, pairs) but generally **no backtested performance numbers, cost models or parameter choices tied to a tested sample**. The Concretum papers are the opposite: one strategy, fixed parameters, full equity curve.

### Inferences
- The Concretum template is stable across papers: 14-day lookbacks for intraday features (ATR, RV, σ_t), a 1%-risk or vol-target sizing rule, a 4x intraday leverage cap (or 200% for daily), and an exit at the close or by trailing stop. This consistency limits the parameter-search freedom the authors had, but the papers do not report pre-registered parameters.
- The two ORB papers use **different entries**: QQQ enters at the 09:35 open with the stop at the opposite end of the first bar; Stocks in Play uses a stop order at the range extreme with a 10%-ATR stop. Coders should not mix them.
- The 10%-ATR stop in the Stocks-in-Play paper is very tight (≈0.1 daily ATR). That makes results highly sensitive to fill assumptions, stop slippage and same-bar entry/stop ordering.

### Gaps
- Primary PDFs could not be read (proxy-blocked). The Stocks-in-Play paper's max DD (12%), hit ratio (48.4%) and timeframe table come from a single secondary source and need checking.
- Exact 1%-risk base (total equity vs per-slot) in the Stocks-in-Play paper is unresolved.
- Beat the Market: paper's own max DD, hit rate, trade count and slippage assumption were not found.
- VWAP Holy Grail paper: bar size, entry timing and the instrument the 9.4% DD / Sharpe 2.1 refer to are unverified.
- Trend-stocks paper: net results, turnover-control algorithm and vol target not found. Industry-trends paper: sizing parameters not found.
- Bitcoin intraday seasonality paper: rules not found.

## Q2. Which rules transfer to Indian markets (Nifty futures, NSE stocks) and crypto perpetuals, and which assumptions break

### Takeaway
The signal logic (first-bar ORB, relative volume, noise bands anchored on max/min(open, prior close), Donchian ensemble) is market-agnostic. What breaks is cost and execution. Indian intraday round-trip costs are ~20 bps including slippage, against an edge of a few bps per trade for the index strategies, and the one public NSE ORB test lost money out of sample. Crypto perps suit the daily Donchian ensemble well, but intraday "session open" strategies need a substitute anchor because crypto trades 24/7.

### Cited Findings
- NSE intraday cost model used in one public test: brokerage ₹20 or 0.03%/order; STT 0.025% on the sell side; exchange charges 0.00297%; SEBI fee 0.0001%; stamp 0.003% on the buy side; GST 18%; slippage 0.05%/side — [md0n-cmd/indian-equity-backtests](https://github.com/md0n-cmd/indian-equity-backtests)
- That repo's ORB test (15-min opening range, relative-volume filter, Nifty 100, 2021–2025): **−21.8% over 4 years**, with ₹173 gross profit on 759 trades against ₹2,357 in costs. It was +10.4% in the single tuned in-sample year and lost money in every out-of-sample year. An intraday momentum test (first 30 min predicting the last 30 min) captured 1.5 bps/trade against ~20 bps of costs — [md0n-cmd/indian-equity-backtests](https://github.com/md0n-cmd/indian-equity-backtests). Caveat: it uses current Nifty 100 constituents (survivorship-biased) and is not a faithful Zarattini replication (15-min range, not 5-min top-20 RV).
- STT on futures sales rose from 0.0125% to **0.02%** from Oct 1 2024. STT on one Nifty futures lot rose from ~₹77 to ~₹123 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/budget/news/budget-2024-steep-stt-increase-to-tame-retail-frenzy-in-derivatives-market-124072301145_1.html); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/new-stt-rules-effective-october-1-important-updates-for-futures-and-options-traders)
- One NSE adaptation of the Stocks-in-Play ORB maps the times to 09:15–09:20 IST for the opening range, an entry window of 09:20–15:25, and exit at 15:25–15:30. It keeps the ₹5 price / 1M-share volume / ₹0.50 ATR thresholds unchanged, suggests a 500k-share volume floor, and notes NSE price bands of ±5/10/20% — [Hummingbird-Project](https://github.com/JittoJoseph/Hummingbird-Project/blob/main/orb_strategy_implementation.md) (secondary; its claim that circuit limits "protect against catastrophic losses" is wrong for a short position caught by an upper circuit, which may be unfillable)
- The SPY noise-area replication on ES futures cost only ~0.4 bps per round trip vs an expectancy of ~2.6 bps/trade, so even in US futures the edge is a few bps per trade — [zarattini-2024-momentum-spy CONCLUSIONS](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- The crypto ensemble is long/flat daily with a 25% vol target. On Binance spot (2017–2026), raw weights 0.25/σ₉₀ had medians of 0.3–0.5 for BTC/ETH/BNB and almost never exceeded 1x, so a **1x cap barely binds** (reviewer's own calculation, not the paper's) — [PR #61](https://github.com/alfred1123/Quant_Strategies/pull/61)
- A Turtle-style breakout on BTC (author-reported, Xueqiu): daily close-confirmation +33.5%/yr (max DD −47%); 4-hour bars −25.3%/yr; 1-hour bars "almost zero" after a 10 bp fee. Breakout trend works on daily bars and fails on faster bars after fees — [as summarized in PR #61](https://github.com/alfred1123/Quant_Strategies/pull/61)

### Inferences
- **Nifty futures + noise area / QQQ-style ORB:** the rules port directly. Use 09:15 IST as the open, previous settlement or close as the gap anchor, checks every 30 minutes from 09:45, and flat by ~15:20–15:25. The 2% daily vol target with a 4x cap must become a whole-number lot count. A lot is a large notional, so small accounts face heavy rounding: sizes jump between 0 and 1–2 lots, which distorts vol targeting. With STT at 0.02% on the sell side plus brokerage, exchange charges and slippage, round-trip cost is likely several bps. That is comparable to or larger than the ~2–3 bps/trade gross expectancy the US replications found, so the edge is likely to be consumed. (Inference; no direct Nifty noise-area backtest was found.)
- **NSE stocks + Stocks-in-Play ORB:** intraday shorting of cash equities is generally possible only intraday (MIS-type products), which matches a strategy that is flat at the close. The thresholds ($5, 1M shares, $0.50 ATR) need rescaling to rupee and turnover terms; a ₹0.50 ATR floor is meaningless for most NSE names. A 10%-of-ATR stop on a ₹1,000 stock with a ₹20 ATR is ₹2 (0.2%). Round-trip costs of ~0.1% including slippage would eat ~0.5R per trade, far more than the +0.08R average edge the paper reports for RV > 1. Price bands and circuit filters create unfillable stops. Leverage is set by broker MIS margin rather than a fixed 4x.
- **Crypto perps:** the Donchian ensemble ports cleanly (daily close, long/flat; shorting is possible but untested in the paper). Perp-specific additions: funding payments (the paper used spot-like returns, so funding on long positions during bull trends is an extra cost the paper does not model), exchange-specific daily close time, and liquidation risk if leverage is used (the paper's 200% cap is moot at a 25% vol target). Intraday ORB/noise-area strategies need an artificial session anchor (e.g. 00:00 UTC or the Asia/US opens). Concretum's own Bitcoin seasonality paper suggests session effects (the Monday Asia open) matter.
- **Leverage caps:** the 4x FINRA intraday cap in the US papers is a binding constraint in their results (QQQ ORB uses it for ~33%/yr). Indian intraday margin (broker-set, regulated by SEBI peak-margin rules) and exchange leverage limits differ, so return levels will not transfer even if the edge does.

### Gaps
- Current Nifty lot size, SEBI MIS margin rules and short-selling specifics were not verified from primary sources in this pass.
- No public replication of the noise-area strategy on Nifty/BankNifty futures, and none of the 5-min top-20 RV ORB on NSE, was found.
- No crypto-perp replication with funding costs was found for the Donchian ensemble.

## Q3. Independent replications and post-publication performance

### Takeaway
The code-level rules replicate: trade counts and Sharpe ratios match the papers closely. But every independent test that extends beyond the papers' samples or adds realistic slippage shows a much weaker edge. The QQQ ORB breaks even at ~2.2¢/share of slippage, with most of its PnL coming from 2022. The SPY noise-area strategy replicated well through 2024 but has had negative Sharpe in 2025–2026. A Stocks-in-Play ORB replicator reported negative expectancy in recent months. The daily crypto trend paper has had no independent rerun yet.

### Cited Findings
- **QQQ ORB replication** (giovannibrusco, Jan 2016–Feb 2023). No slippage: 1,775 trades vs the paper's 1,795; Sharpe 1.06 vs 1.12; CAGR 30.4%; max DD 22.4%; $0.070/share gross edge. With $0.02/share entry slippage and $0.04/share stop slippage: net PnL $4,860 vs $138,639; Sharpe 0.23; CAGR 2.7%; max DD 43.9%. **Break-even slippage is ~2.2¢/share.** 2022 accounts for 38% of the replication PnL and 76% of an NQ-filtered variant's PnL. QQQ buy-and-hold had Sharpe 0.72 and CAGR 15.3% over the same period — [zarattini-2023-orb-qqq](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- **SPY noise-area replication** (giovannibrusco/codecat-ops; SPY via Alpaca IEX Jul 2020–Jul 2026; ES via IB May 2024–Jul 2026; paper's $0.0035/share commission):
  - Final exit: Sharpe 1.11, CAGR 15.9%, max DD −24.2%, alpha 16.7% (t = 2.85), beta −0.06, 1,379 trades, win rate 41%, payoff 1.69, +2.58 bps/trade.
  - Base exit: Sharpe 0.86, win rate 57%.
  - By year: 2020–2024 Sharpe 1.45–2.03 each year, including **+25.8% in 2022 vs SPY −19.5%**. Then **2025 −4.9% (Sharpe −0.27)** and **2026 YTD −12.9% (Sharpe −1.91)**.
  - ES and SPY daily returns correlate at 0.97, so the decline is not a data artefact.
  - Slippage of $0.01/share cuts Sharpe from 1.11 to 0.94.
  - A disciplined 27-variant Maróy-style grid selected the paper's original configuration (14-day lookback, 30-min checks, final exit). Quarterly walk-forward reselection lowered Sharpe to 0.57 vs 0.92 for the fixed configuration.
  - — [zarattini-2024-momentum-spy README/CONCLUSIONS/validation](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Quantitativo ES replication (Jan 2025): ~+2 bps/trade, win rate ~36%, payoff ~2.1 (as quoted in the SPEC above; the original page was not accessible) — [SPEC citing Quantitativo](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [Quantitativo post](https://www.quantitativo.com/p/intraday-momentum-for-es-and-nq)

### Inferences
- The intraday papers' edges are a few bps per trade (QQQ ORB ~7¢/share gross; SPY noise area ~2–4 bps). They are therefore *cost- and regime-dependent*: profits cluster in high-volatility years (2020, 2022), and both papers' samples end before the 2025–2026 decay seen in replications.
- Of the Concretum family, the daily trend strategies (crypto Donchian ensemble, ATH stocks, industry trends) are the most robust to costs (10 bps/trade, low turnover). They also transfer best to Indian/crypto settings, but they lack independent post-publication tests.
- For a coder, "frozen" paper parameters plus forward monitoring is the defensible approach. The SPY replication shows that parameter tuning and walk-forward reselection both reduced performance.

### Gaps
- No independent net-of-slippage replication of the Stocks-in-Play ORB with visible numbers was accessible. The MQL5 and QuantConnect results could not be read.
- No independent post-2025 data was found for the crypto ensemble, ATH trend-following, or industry trends.
- Whether the 2025–2026 noise-area drawdown is a regime pause or a permanent decay cannot be determined; the replicator says it needs pre-2020 data or more time.
