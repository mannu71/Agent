## Q1. Which intraday rules show positive, out-of-sample, after-cost results? (evidence table per strategy)

### Takeaway
No intraday rule has independent, post-publication, net-of-cost confirmation in Indian markets. The strongest-supported effects are structural, not chart-based: (a) end-of-day market intraday momentum (first or "rest-of-day" return predicts the last 30 minutes), which has a mechanism (gamma and leveraged-ETF hedging) and evidence across many markets, but small per-trade edges and signs of decay; (b) the overnight-vs-intraday return decomposition, which is a very large and persistent effect in India. Opening-range breakout and VWAP-trend results come from practitioner authors with commercial ties, and the one independent replication found shows ORB gross results reproduce but net results are about zero.

### Inferences
- The only intraday components with both a mechanism and multi-market support are (1) end-of-day market momentum and (2) the overnight/intraday split; both are index-level, and the swing composite should take them as context features rather than as standalone trades.
- ORB and VWAP-trend results are best treated as unreplicated marketing-grade backtests: the one independent replication found zero net edge, and both rely on commission-only cost models in the US where commissions are near zero.
- RVOL is not an alpha signal on its own but appears to be the ingredient that makes ORB work in the published sample; it is a reasonable "attention" filter for a composite.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the full Zarattini ORB paper to confirm average R per trade, slippage assumption or whether the top-20 RVOL selection used only data available at 09:35.
- No post-2024 out-of-sample test of Zarattini ORB on US stocks (rather than index futures) was found.
- No peer-reviewed gap-fill or gap-and-go net-of-cost study was found for any market.
- The India hedging-demand intraday-momentum paper remains unrefereed; no 2023–2026 Indian journal replication was found.

## Q2. What do SEBI's studies say about retail intraday profitability?

### Takeaway
SEBI's equity-cash intraday study (July 2024, FY23 data) found 71% of individual intraday traders lost money, rising to 80% among those making more than 500 trades a year; frequency, not skill, is the strongest correlate of loss. The FY26 F&O study (already summarised in the blueprint: 87.7% lost after costs) shows costs alone flip a large share of gross winners into net losers.

### Cited Findings
- 71% of individual intraday traders in the equity cash segment made losses in FY23; average loss ₹5,371 per trader — [Motilal Oswal summary of SEBI study](https://www.motilaloswal.com/learning-centre/2024/7/sebi-study-reveals-key-insights-on-individual-intraday-traders-in-india); [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/?p=422657)
- Loss-makers rose to 80% among traders with more than 500 trades a year; under-30 traders had 76% loss-makers — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/most-intra-day-traders-incur-losses-half-of-traders-under-30-years-herere-detailed-findings-from-sebi-report)
- Intraday participation rose ~300% between FY19 and FY23; under-30 share rose from 18% to 48% — [Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/learning-centre/2024/7/sebi-study-reveals-key-insights-on-individual-intraday-traders-in-india)
- Sample: clients of the top-10 brokers, ~86% of individual equity-cash clients in FY23 — [Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/learning-centre/2024/7/sebi-study-reveals-key-insights-on-individual-intraday-traders-in-india)
- FY26 F&O: 82.1% lost before costs, 87.7% after; options ~92% of losses (see blueprint) — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rs91685-cr-lost-88-of-individual-traders-lost-money-in-fy26-options-drove-92-of-losses-550474-2026-08-21)

### Inferences
- The 71% → 80% gradient with trade count implies that cost drag plus overtrading, not lack of signals, is the binding constraint. A composite should cap intraday decisions (e.g. one index decision a day, or intraday used only to time multi-day entries).

### Gaps
- No SEBI study of intraday cash traders using FY25 or FY26 data was found; the FY23 study predates the April 2026 STT change (which did not alter cash intraday STT).

## Q3. Realistic Indian round-trip costs for intraday equity and futures; minimum edge per trade

### Takeaway
After the April 2026 changes, an intraday round trip costs roughly 4.5–11 bps in NSE cash (depending on ticket size, because brokerage is capped at ₹20/order) and about 6 bps in Nifty futures (dominated by 0.05% sell-side STT), before slippage. Adding 2–10 bps of spread/slippage, a rule needs an average gross edge of roughly 10–20 bps per trade to be worth running at 2× cost safety, which is above the published average per-trade edge of the US intraday-momentum strategy.

### Cited Findings
- Intraday equity STT 0.025% sell side only; futures STT 0.05% sell side only from 1 Apr 2026 (was 0.02%) — [Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated); [stockcalc](https://stockcalc.in/blog/zerodha-brokerage-charges-2026-full-breakdown)
- NSE exchange transaction charge ~0.00297% (equity) and ~0.00173% (futures); Zerodha brokerage ₹20 or 0.03% per order, whichever is lower — [stockcalc](https://stockcalc.in/blog/zerodha-brokerage-charges-2026-full-breakdown)
- Zerodha charges ₹40 instead of ₹20 per intraday F&O order when cash-margin shortfall exceeds ₹5 lakh (blueprint) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/zerodha-raises-fo-charges-to-%E2%82%B940-per-order-for-certain-intraday-trades/)
- Note author's arithmetic (Zerodha rates above, SEBI fee ₹10/crore, stamp 0.003% buy for intraday equity and 0.002% buy for futures, GST 18% on brokerage+exchange+SEBI fees), statutory+brokerage only, excluding slippage:

| Trade | Round-trip cost | bps of notional |
|---|---|---|
| Intraday cash, ₹25,000 | ₹26.5 | 10.6 |
| Intraday cash, ₹1 lakh | ₹82.4 | 8.2 |
| Intraday cash, ₹5 lakh | ₹223 | 4.5 |
| Nifty futures, ₹15 lakh notional | ₹892 | 6.0 (≈15 Nifty points at 25,000) |
| Nifty futures, ₹30 lakh | ₹1,737 | 5.8 |

- Stamp-duty and NSE-charge figures conflict across extracts (blueprint flags 0.003% vs 0.015% stamp and 0.00297% vs 0.00689% NSE charge); the table uses the lower intraday rates — [blueprint source list](https://stockcalc.in/blog/zerodha-brokerage-charges-2026-full-breakdown)
- US benchmark: Gao et al. timing earned 6.3% a year gross across roughly 250 trading days (Jan 1999–Dec 2012) — [CXO Advisory](https://cxoadvisory.com/calendar-effects/first-and-last-half-hours-of-trading-linked)

### Inferences
- 6.3% a year over ~250 days is about 2.5 bps per trading day on average gross (note author's arithmetic), well below a 6 bps Nifty futures round trip. Even if the Indian effect were twice as large, an unconditional daily last-half-hour trade would fail; only conditional versions (large first-hour move, high-volatility or news days, where the cited studies say the effect concentrates) can plausibly clear costs. This is consistent with the blueprint's "2× cost" switch-on rule.
- For intraday cash, ticket size matters: below ~₹67,000 per order the ₹20 cap is not hit and brokerage is 0.03% a side, so small accounts pay ~10 bps before slippage.
- Minimum gross edge for go-live: ~12 bps (futures) and ~10–20 bps (cash, by size) per trade including 2–5 bps slippage and a 2× safety factor would push it to ~20–30 bps (inference, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No published measurement of realised retail slippage on Nifty futures or NSE F&O stocks at the open/close was found; the 2–10 bps range is an assumption.
- Current Nifty futures lot size after 2025–26 revisions was not verified in this session.

## Q4. Do intraday signals improve the ENTRY TIMING of multi-day swing trades? (open vs VWAP vs close; Indian night effect) and SEBI rule effects

### Takeaway
Yes, indirectly and mostly through the overnight/intraday split: in India and the US, index and momentum returns accrue overnight while intraday returns are flat to negative, which favours entering swing longs late in the session (near the close) rather than at the open, and exiting near the open. No study directly tests "buy the open vs VWAP vs close" for Indian swing trades. SEBI's 2024–26 F&O changes mainly affect options and position monitoring, but they push edge away from high-turnover intraday trading.

### Cited Findings
- SENSEX 1997–2021: overnight-only ₹1 → ₹12,171; intraday-only −99.89% — [Knuteson, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.00223)
- Momentum profits accrue overnight; other anomalies' profits accrue intraday (US, 14 strategies) — [Lou, Polk & Skouras, LSE](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/87481)
- Nifty intraday: significant "open jump" and "persistent end of session" effects — [i-scholar AJM](https://i-scholar.in/index.php/Ajm/article/view/179374)
- Nifty overnight short-option returns positive and highly significant 2017–2020; intraday not — [vLex](https://law-journals-books.vlex.com/vid/the-asymmetry-in-day-1115554990)
- Indian overnight firm return is a sentiment proxy with short-term persistence — [CiteDrive / Vision journal](https://www.citedrive.com/en/discovery/make-some-noise-tonightovernight-return-as-investor-sentiment-index-indian-evidence)
- Intraday momentum reverts over the following days (Baltussen et al.), i.e. a strong last-half-hour push is partly reversed later — [Erasmus](https://pure.eur.nl/en/publications/hedging-demand-and-market-intraday-momentum/)
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/CIR/P/2025/122 (1 Sep 2025, effective 1 Oct 2025): intraday index-option positions monitored on a futures-equivalent/delta basis, net limit ₹5,000 crore and gross ₹10,000 crore per entity, at least four random intraday snapshots with emphasis near the close; expiry-day breach penalties from 6 Dec 2025 — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/equity/sebi-outlines-new-framework-for-monitoring-of-intraday-position-limits-for-index-derivatives); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-tightens-intraday-position-limits-derivatives.html)
- Other 2024–26 rules (one weekly expiry per exchange, larger contract sizes, upfront premium, futures STT 0.02% → 0.05%) are covered in the blueprint — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/budget-2026-fm-sitharaman-raises-stt-on-fo-trades-signals-tighter-market-oversight-513932-2026-02-01)

### Inferences
- Candidate components for the swing+intraday composite, ranked by evidence strength:
  1. **Overnight/intraday decomposition (strong):** enter momentum swing longs in the last 30–60 minutes rather than at the 09:15 open; compute each stock's rolling overnight vs intraday return as a sentiment/attention feature.
  2. **Market intraday momentum state (moderate, decaying):** sign and size of Nifty's prev-close→14:45 (or first-hour) return as an end-of-day timing filter — e.g. delay a swing entry when the market is down hard into the close on a high-volatility day, since the last-half-hour push tends to continue then partially revert next day.
  3. **RVOL (moderate as a filter):** require above-normal early volume to trust a breakout-type swing entry; avoid entries on thin days.
  4. **Opening-gap context (weak):** treat large gaps as information about the overnight leg already paid; do not chase a gap at the open (practitioner evidence that large gaps often retrace; no net-of-cost study).
  5. **VWAP (execution only):** use VWAP or a price-below-VWAP condition to reduce implementation cost of a swing entry, not as a directional signal.
  6. **ORB, VWAP-cross trading, OFI (exclude):** unreplicated net edge (ORB, VWAP-cross) or horizon/data too short (OFI).
- The Oct 2025 delta-based intraday monitoring, with snapshots weighted toward the close, could reduce large-player gamma-hedging flows near the close in index options, which may weaken the end-of-day momentum mechanism in India (speculative; untested).

### Gaps
- No Indian study comparing open, VWAP and close entries for multi-day holds was found.
- No post-2024 Indian measurement of overnight vs intraday Nifty return split was found from a primary source (practitioner pages from multibagg.ai appeared in search but could not be verified).
- No evidence yet on whether the April 2026 futures STT increase changed intraday futures volumes or the strength of end-of-day momentum in Nifty.
