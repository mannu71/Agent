Method note: most primary-source pages (bis.org, arxiv.org, taxguru.in, cryptotimes.io, yahoo finance) were blocked by this environment's egress proxy, so many findings below rest on search-engine extracts of those pages, not full-text reads. ## 1. Indian crypto taxation (115BBH / 194S, the new Income-tax Act 2025, GST, derivatives)

### Takeaway
Budget 2026 (Feb 2026) left the regime unchanged: 30% flat tax plus 4% cess (and surcharge) on VDA transfer gains. No losses can be set off or carried forward, and 1% TDS applies on transfers. The new Income-tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 Apr 2026. It renumbers these rules (TDS moves to s.393) and adds exchange transaction reporting (old s.285BAA, new s.509, aligned with CARF). Spot crypto is therefore very hostile to high-turnover intraday trading. INR-settled crypto futures are widely marketed as outside 115BBH and TDS, but that treatment is not settled law.

### Cited Findings
- Budget 2026 left s.115BBH unchanged. The 30% flat rate, 1% TDS under s.194S and the no-set-off rule continue as before — [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/crypto-vda-tax-india-30-percent-194s/); [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/india-keeps-30-crypto-tax-as-new-vda-reporting-rules-take-effect)
- VDA income = sale consideration minus cost of acquisition only (no other deductions such as fees). It is taxed at a flat 30% plus surcharge and cess. Losses cannot be set off or carried forward — [Legal Service India](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/crypto-taxation-india-vda-tax-30-percent-tds-guide/); [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/crypto-vda-tax-india-30-percent-194s/)
- The 30% rate carries an extra 4% cess — [TaxGuru (IT Bill 2025)](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/taxation-virtual-digital-assets-cryptocurrencies-income-tax-bill-2025.html)
- 1% TDS under s.194S applies to payments for VDA transfers above ₹50,000/yr for "specified persons" (e.g., non-audit individuals/HUFs), or ₹10,000/yr for others. On Indian exchanges the exchange deducts and deposits it. In P2P trades the buyer deducts. TDS is credited against final tax liability — [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/crypto-vda-tax-india-30-percent-194s/)
- Under the Income-tax Act 2025, VDA TDS moves from s.194S to s.393(8)(vi) at the same 1% rate. One source says the new law "removes the minimum threshold", but the same source then restates the ₹50,000 / ₹10,000 thresholds. This is internally contradictory and unverified — [TaxGuru 1961 vs 2025](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/crypto-vda-taxation-income-tax-act-1961-vs-2025-framework.html); [CAclubindia](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/tds-on-virtual-digital-assets-under-income-tax-act-2025-key-rules-rate-threshold-and-compliance-26748.asp)
- The VDA definition (s.2(47A) of the 1961 Act) was widened by Finance Act 2025 with a new sub-clause (d) explicitly covering "crypto-asset". It carries into the 2025 Act — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/crypto-vda-taxation-income-tax-act-1961-vs-2025-framework.html)
- The Income-tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 Apr 2026. Exchanges must report customer transactions in a standardised format from Apr 2026, under s.285BAA (old Act) / s.509 (new Act). Penalties are ₹200/day for late reporting and ₹50,000 for inaccurate information. India is listed among 52 "relevant" CARF jurisdictions — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/india-keeps-30-crypto-tax-as-new-vda-reporting-rules-take-effect); [CryptoTimes, 21 Sep 2026](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/21/india-crypto-tax-2026-30-vda-levy-1-tds-stay-as-section-285baa-reporting-goes-live/)
- A Budget 2025 proposal brought VDAs into block-assessment/undisclosed-income provisions. Press reported a possible "60% tax penalty" on undisclosed crypto income — [Followin](https://followin.io/en/feed/16047817); [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2508973989)
- GST: Delta Exchange India states that 18% GST applies on all trading fees — [Tradelize review of Delta](https://tradelize.com/crypto-exchange-reviews/delta-exchange-review/) (secondary)
- Derivatives: platforms and some advisers say INR-settled crypto futures and options are not VDA transfers, so neither 1% TDS nor 30% s.115BBH applies, and profits are taxed as (speculative or non-speculative) business income at slab rates — [Delta Exchange India user guide](https://guides.delta.exchange/delta-exchange-india-user-guide); [CoinSwitch F&O tax](https://coinswitch.co/switch/crypto-futures-derivatives/crypto-futures-options-tax/)
- Other practitioners call the classification unsettled ("Business income or 30% VDA tax?") — [CA in Delhi](https://www.caindelhiindia.com/blog/crypto-futures-taxation/); [RJA](https://carajput.com/blog/crypto-futures-options-in-india/)

### Inferences
- The no-set-off rule is applied per transaction or asset in practice. An intraday spot strategy is then taxed on gross winning trades while losing trades give no relief. Example: a strategy with ₹10L gross wins and ₹9L gross losses (net ₹1L) could owe about 31.2% × ₹10L ≈ ₹3.1L, which is far more than its net profit. Spot-crypto intraday strategies with a hit-rate below about 80–90% are structurally unviable after tax. (This depends on how strictly no-set-off is applied within the same asset. Many CAs net gains and losses within the same coin, but losses across coins clearly cannot offset. Verify with a CA.)
- 1% TDS is a cash-flow drag, not a final cost, because it is refundable or creditable. For high turnover it is crippling. Each sale withholds 1% of the proceeds, so about 100 sell trades consumes 100% of the capital in TDS until the ITR refund, which can be months to a year later. HFT, market-making and cross-exchange arbitrage on Indian spot books are effectively impossible.
- The only plausibly viable intraday crypto venue for an Indian resident is INR-settled perpetual futures on FIU-registered Indian platforms (Delta India, CoinDCX, CoinSwitch, Mudrex, ZebPay). Even there the tax position is contested: if the authorities later apply s.115BBH to derivatives, past returns could be re-taxed at 30% without loss set-off. Fee cost should include the 18% GST on fees.

### Gaps
- Could not read CBDT notifications or the text of the Income-tax Act 2025 directly (egress blocked). The exact s.393 threshold wording and any CBDT circular on crypto derivatives remain unverified.
- No CBDT/ITAT ruling found that definitively classifies INR-settled crypto perps.
- Whether GST (18%) applies to the VDA transaction value itself (as opposed to fees) was not found in authoritative sources.

## 2. Exchanges legally usable by Indian residents (FIU-IND status, perps, fees, leverage, APIs)

### Takeaway
Only FIU-IND-registered VDASPs are lawful for residents. About 54 were registered by mid-2026. Binance (₹18.82 cr penalty, 2024), Bybit (₹9.27 cr penalty, Jan 2025) and KuCoin re-entered after registering. OKX exited in 2024. In Oct 2025 FIU sent notices to 25 more offshore platforms and sought URL blocks. Indian-native perp venues with APIs are Delta Exchange India, CoinDCX, CoinSwitch, Mudrex and ZebPay. WazirX restarted after a Singapore-court scheme in Oct 2025. Funding offshore margin accounts via LRS is prohibited.

### Cited Findings

- Binance and KuCoin won FIU registration in May 2024 — [CoinDesk, 10 May 2024](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2024/05/10/binance-kucoin-win-registration-with-indias-financial-intelligence-unit). - CoinDCX lost $44.3M from an internal operational hot wallet on 19 Jul 2025, drained within about 5 minutes. The company said user funds were unaffected and covered the loss from reserves — [CoinDesk, 19 Jul 2025](https://www.coindesk.com/web3/2025/07/19/indian-crypto-exchange-coindcx-suffers-44m-hack); [Halborn](https://www.halborn.com/blog/post/explained-the-coindcx-hack-july-2025)

Has a public futures API — [TradersUnion](https://tradersunion.com/brokers/crypto/view/coindcx/fees/); [CoinDCX blog](https://coindcx.com/blog/crypto-futures-trading/10-reasons-to-choose-crypto-futures-trading-on-coindcx/); [CoinDCX futures API docs](https://coindcx.com/documentation/crypto-futures); conflicting leverage figure from [TradingFinder](https://tradingfinder.com/exchanges/coindcx/)
- **CoinSwitch**: futures at about 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker (USDT-margined). Launched INR-margined crypto futures and INR crypto options. Spot is INR pairs (CoinSwitch PRO) — [CoinSwitch futures fees](https://coinswitch.co/switch/crypto-futures-derivatives/crypto-future-transaction-fees/); [Entrepreneur India](https://www.entrepreneur.com/en-in/news-and-trends/coinswitch-introduces-inr-based-crypto-futures-trading/490782); [CoinSwitch options](https://coinswitch.co/switch/crypto/inr-crypto-options-trading/)
- **ccxt support** (checked in the ccxt source, master on 1 Oct 2026): native modules exist for `delta`, `mudrex` (countries IN; swap only; rateLimit 100 ms ≈ 10 req/s; endpoint trade.mudrex.com/fapi/v1), `zebpay` (IN; spot + swap; rateLimit 50 ms; spot sapi.zebpay.com, futures futuresbe.zebpay.com) and `bitbns` (IN; spot only; rateLimit 1000 ms). ### Inferences
- For a legal, API-driven intraday agent, the realistic shortlist is Delta Exchange India (best documented API, INR-settled perps, TradingView integration), CoinDCX Futures (custom REST/websocket client needed), and Mudrex or ZebPay futures (ccxt-supported but thinner liquidity).
- Binance and Bybit are FIU-registered and therefore legal to use. But funding them requires INR on-ramps or P2P USDT (with TDS, and the USDT premium below), and funding offshore margin via LRS is prohibited. Their regulatory and banking-access position for Indian residents should be checked at deployment.
- Counterparty risk is concrete: two of the largest Indian venues suffered hot-wallet hacks within 12 months (WazirX July 2024, user funds frozen about 15 months, roughly 15% haircut deferred into tokens; CoinDCX July 2025, covered by reserves). Diversify across venues and sweep profits to the bank frequently.

### Gaps
- The official FIU-IND registered-entity list could not be fetched. Giottus registration status and current fee schedules were not independently confirmed.
- Delta India's documented REST rate limits and websocket limits were not retrieved (docs site blocked). CoinDCX API rate limits were also not retrieved.
- Whether Binance or Bybit offer perpetual futures to Indian KYC users post-registration was not confirmed.

## 3. Crypto market structure (24/7, funding, basis, liquidation cascades, counterparty and stablecoin risk)

### Takeaway
Perp funding has compressed. In 2024–25 BTC funding sat mostly at the 0.01%/8h "baseline" (≈10.95% annualized on Binance). By Sep 2026 the cross-exchange average was only about +2.1%/yr. Funding spikes in bull, retail-driven phases and goes negative in sell-offs. Liquidation cascades can be extreme: on 10 Oct 2025, $19B was liquidated in hours (85% longs) and Hyperliquid triggered ADL. The Indian USDT premium (normally 3–5%, spiking to 8.5–10% in mid-2026) is a structural, non-arbitrageable distortion for residents.

### Cited Findings
- In 2024–25 the average BTC funding rate hovered at the 0.01%/8h baseline, which is the default interest component when perp ≈ spot. Early-cycle bull markets saw >0.2%/8h (over 200% annualized) — [BitMEX Q3 2025 derivatives report](https://www.bitmex.com/blog/2025q3-derivatives-report)
- On BitMEX, BTC perp funding printed exactly 0.01% for 78.19% of Q3 2025 and was positive more than 92% of the time — [Coincub](https://coincub.com/blog/perpetual-futures-funding-rates-78-of-a-quarter-at-0-01/); [BitMEX](https://www.bitmex.com/blog/2025q3-derivatives-report)
- Binance's 0.01%/8h plateau annualizes to 10.95%. As of 25 Sep 2026, BTC/ETH funding averaged about +2.1%/yr across six exchanges, ranging from −4.9% (Kraken) to +10.0% (Hyperliquid) — [DefiRate perp funding](https://defirate.com/perp/)
- Live and historical funding data sources: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/data/crypto-markets/futures/btc-funding-rates), [Glassnode](https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.FuturesFundingRatePerpetualAll?a=BTC), [CF Benchmarks Kraken Funding Rate Index](https://www.cfbenchmarks.com/data/indices/KFRI), [Kaggle funding dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ramkrijal/perpetuals-funding-rates)
- 10 Oct 2025: more than $19B of leveraged positions were liquidated within hours after the 100% China-tariff announcement, about 9x any previous day. BTC fell about 14% (~$122k → ~$105k) and ETH about 12%. Hyperliquid had $10.08B (53%), Bybit $4.58B and Binance $2.31B. 1.64–1.67M traders were hit, $16.79B long vs $2.49B short. Hyperliquid triggered ADL for the first time in over two years — [Insights4VC](https://insights4vc.substack.com/p/inside-the-19b-flash-crash); [Cointelegraph Magazine](https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/19b-crypto-market-crash-leverage-china-tariffs); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/newsletter/defi-daily/the-ultimate-10-10-crash-autopsy)
- India USDT premium: normally 3–5% over the bank USD rate, spiking to 8.5–10%. In late June 2026 USDT was ₹102.88 vs a bank rate of ₹94.65 (>8.5%). Arbitrage is blocked by FEMA/LRS limits, 1% TDS, 30% tax and P2P banking risk — [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/usdt-premium-india-8-5-percent-supply/); [DailyCoin](https://dailycoin.com/india-usdt-premium-surges-above-8-5-after-crypto-on-ramp-disruption/); [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/92d08-the-india-premium-why-usdt-costs-more-than-a-dollar-in-india); [Parameter](https://parameter.io/usdt-premium-surges-to-10-on-indian-crypto-platforms-amid-supply-crunch/)
- Exchange counterparty events: WazirX ($230M, Jul 2024, 15-month freeze) and CoinDCX ($44M, Jul 2025) — see Section 2 citations; also [Wikipedia: 2024 WazirX hack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WazirX_hack)

### Inferences
- With funding near zero to 0.01%/8h most of the time, funding is a small carry. It matters mostly during sentiment extremes: high positive funding in euphoric rallies, negative in sharp sell-offs. Funding can be used as a sentiment/crowding filter for intraday signals.
- Cascades like 10/10 mean that stops can gap, ADL can close profitable hedges, and leverage must be very low (≤2–3x) for a "low-risk" agent. 24/7 trading means the agent needs unattended risk controls such as exchange-side stop orders and kill switches.
- INR-margined Indian perps embed USDT/INR pricing choices. Check whether a platform marks USDT at the premium rate or the bank rate, since the 3–10% gap can distort P&L in INR terms.

### Gaps
- No authoritative long-run average for ETH funding was found separately from BTC. Funding data specific to Indian exchanges (Delta India, CoinDCX) was not found.
- Stablecoin-specific risk evidence (USDT reserve/depeg history, USDC Mar 2023 depeg) was not researched in this pass.

## 4. Empirical evidence on intraday strategies and carry/arbitrage

### Takeaway
Academic evidence supports (a) a large but shrinking crypto cash-and-carry premium (BIS WP 1087: average about 7–8%/yr, spiking to 60%; Sharpe collapsed from 2024 and turned negative in 2025 per one extract), (b) intraday time-series momentum in BTC (first half-hour predicts last half-hour), and (c) strong 15-minute directional mean reversion across Binance pairs (2026 arXiv). For Indian residents, carry and arbitrage are largely closed off. Spot legs incur TDS and 30% tax without loss offset, and cross-border arbitrage is barred by FEMA, so only futures-only intraday strategies remain plausible.

### Cited Findings
- BIS WP 1087 "Crypto carry" (Schmeling, Schrimpf, Todorov; Apr 2023, revised Oct 2025). Carry, the return on long spot / short futures, can reach 60% p.a. It is persistent and spiky, averaging about 7–8%/yr depending on asset and contract (an earlier version said ">10% annually"). It is driven by trend-chasing retail demand for leveraged long exposure plus scarce arbitrage capital. Interest-rate differentials explain little. High carry predicts future crashes — [BIS WP 1087](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry); [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4268371); [CEPR DP20719](https://cepr.org/publications/dp20719); [VoxEU column](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/crypto-carry-market-segmentation-and-price-distortions-digital-asset-markets)
- Retail micro-futures (2021) raised carry, while US spot BTC ETFs (2024) cut carry by about 3 pp across exchanges and about 5 pp on CME — [CEPR/VoxEU](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/crypto-carry-market-segmentation-and-price-distortions-digital-asset-markets) (via search extract)
- A crypto carry strategy had an annualized Sharpe of 6.45 over 2020–2025, falling to 4.06 from 2024 and turning negative in 2025. These figures came from a search extract, possibly from [arXiv 2510.14435 "Cryptocurrency as an Investable Asset Class: Coming of Age"](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14435v4) rather than BIS. Attribution unverified.
- He, Manela, Ross & von Wachter, "Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures": perp deviations from no-arbitrage prices are much larger than in FX, comove across coins, and **diminish over time** as markets mature. A simple strategy earns large Sharpe ratios even at Binance's highest fee tier — [arXiv 2212.06888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888v6)
- Bitcoin intraday time-series momentum: the first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour, in-sample and out-of-sample, with economic gains especially in downturns — [Univ. of Reading accepted version (2021)](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/100181/3/21Sep2021Bitcoin%20Intraday%20Time-Series%20Momentum.R2.pdf)
- Short-horizon mean reversion (arXiv 2608.21888, Aug 2026): at 15-minute horizons, 90% of 183 Binance pairs show significant directional reversal vs 2.7% of 187 US stocks/ETFs. The signal is in signs, not magnitudes (lag-1 autocorrelation ≈ 0 on majors, but betting against the previous candle captures most of the effect) — [arXiv 2608.21888](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21888v1). Not read in full, so cost-adjusted profitability is unknown.
- Large, liquid coins show short-term momentum, while small and mid-cap coins show reversal driven by illiquidity — [ResearchGate: "Up or down?"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354881251_Up_or_down_Short-term_reversal_momentum_and_liquidity_effects_in_cryptocurrency_markets)
- Practitioner backtests of BTC trend-following vs mean-reversion — [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/revisiting-trend-following-and-mean-reversion-strategies-in-bitcoin/) (non-peer-reviewed)

### Inferences
- Cash-and-carry for an Indian resident: the spot leg on an Indian exchange incurs 1% TDS and 30% tax on gains with no offset against the futures leg. The futures leg is taxed as business income or is contested. Locking about 7–8% gross (often lower in 2025–26, given funding of about 2%/yr in Sep 2026) is not worth it after tax and TDS lock-up. Futures-vs-futures carry (calendar spreads, or perp vs dated futures on the same INR venue) avoids VDA transfers but depends on product availability. Not verified.
- Cross-exchange arbitrage (India vs offshore) is effectively infeasible and arguably illegal. LRS bars margin remittances, the USDT premium persists precisely because arbitrage is blocked, and TDS hits every spot sell. Arbitrage between Indian futures venues (e.g., Delta vs CoinDCX perp price or funding gaps) is the only legal variant. With taker fees of about 0.05–0.07% plus 18% GST per side, it needs gaps above about 0.15–0.2% round trip.
- The 15-minute reversal evidence is attractive, but round-trip taker cost on Indian perps (about 0.12–0.17% including GST) likely exceeds typical 15-minute reversal magnitudes on majors. Maker-only execution would be needed.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the cost-adjusted returns in arXiv 2608.21888 or the post-2023 decay figures for funding-rate arbitrage beyond the extracts above.
- No study was found that measures arbitrage across Indian exchanges or the profitability of INR-perp strategies.

## 5. Data sources and APIs

### Takeaway
Use ccxt for Mudrex, ZebPay, Bitbns and (with an endpoint override) Delta. Write custom clients for CoinDCX and Delta India websockets. Use offshore public market data (Binance, Bybit, funding aggregators) for research signals, since Indian venues have short histories and thin books.

### Cited Findings
- ccxt modules and rate-limit defaults for Indian exchanges: Mudrex 100 ms, ZebPay 50 ms, Bitbns 1000 ms, Delta 300 ms (global host). No CoinDCX, WazirX or CoinSwitch modules, and no ccxt-pro websocket support for any Indian venue — [ccxt source](https://github.com/ccxt/ccxt/tree/master/ts/src)
- Delta Exchange India API base: api.india.delta.exchange — [Delta API docs](https://docs.delta.exchange/)
- CoinDCX publishes futures API documentation — [CoinDCX API docs](https://coindcx.com/documentation/crypto-futures)
- Mudrex API docs: docs.trade.mudrex.com — [ccxt mudrex.ts](https://github.com/ccxt/ccxt/blob/master/ts/src/mudrex.ts)
