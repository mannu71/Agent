Method note: WebFetch was blocked for almost all primary domains in this environment (sebi.gov.in, nseindia/zerodha.com, kite.trade, fyers.in, medianama, broker blogs). Findings below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages (SEBI press-release URLs, Business Standard, Moneylife, Business Today, Outlook Money, broker help pages, etc.). ## 1. SEBI studies on retail trader outcomes (F&O and intraday cash)

### Takeaway
Every SEBI study since 2023 finds that roughly 9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money: 89% in FY22, 93% over FY22-FY24, 91% in FY25 and 87.7% in FY26. In the intraday cash segment about 7 in 10 lose (71% in FY23), and the most frequent traders (more than 500 trades a year) and young traders do worst. Transaction costs alone turn about 4.4 lakh gross-profitable F&O traders into net loss-makers each year, which is the most important single constraint on any intraday design.

### Cited Findings
**Jan 2023 study (FY22)**
- 89% of individual equity F&O traders lost money in FY22, with an average loss of about Rs 1.1 lakh. 90% of *active* traders (more than Rs 1 lakh loss bucket context) lost an average of Rs 1.25 lakh. Only 11% made a profit, averaging Rs 1.5 lakh. The sample was all individual clients of the top-10 brokers, which together made up 67% of individual F&O turnover — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/sebi-study-suggests-89-retail-traders-in-equity-f-o-suffered-losses-123012501466_1.html); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/90-percentage-individual-traders-in-equity-fo-segment-incurred-losses-sebi-research/69634.html)

**Sept 2024 study (FY22-FY24, released 23 Sep 2024)**
- 93% of more than 1 crore individual F&O traders lost money between FY22 and FY24. The average loss was about Rs 2 lakh per trader including transaction costs, and aggregate losses exceeded Rs 1.8 lakh crore over the three years — [SEBI press release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/sep-2024/updated-sebi-study-reveals-93-of-individual-traders-incurred-losses-in-equity-fando-between-fy22-and-fy24-aggregate-losses-exceed-1-8-lakh-crores-over-three-years_86906.html); [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/93-of-1-crore-fo-traders-saw-rs-2l-avg-loss-while-top-3-5-faced-rs-28l-avg-loss-incl-costs-sebi/)
- The top 3.5% of loss-makers (about 4 lakh traders) lost an average of Rs 28 lakh each. Only 1% of individuals earned more than Rs 1 lakh after costs. In FY24, prop traders made gross profits of Rs 33,000 crore and FPIs made Rs 28,000 crore. Individuals spent about Rs 26,000 each on transaction costs in FY24. More than 75% of loss-makers kept trading after successive loss years — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/93-of-1-crore-fo-traders-saw-rs-2l-avg-loss-while-top-3-5-faced-rs-28l-avg-loss-incl-costs-sebi/)

**July 2025 study (FY25)**
- More than 91% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY25. Net losses were Rs 1,05,603 crore, up 41% from Rs 74,812 crore in FY24. The average net loss rose to about Rs 1.1 lakh from Rs 86,728. The sample was the top 13 brokers, covering about 96 lakh unique F&O traders. Unique individual traders fell from 61.4 lakh in Q1 FY25 to 42.7 lakh in Q4 FY25, which the study attributes partly to the Nov 2024 curbs — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/91-percentage-of-retail-traders-lost-money-in-derivatives-losses-in-fo-surged-41-percentage-to-rs105-lakh-crore-in-fy2425-sebi-study/77613.html)

**Aug 2026 study, "Profitability of Individual Traders in the Equity Derivatives Segment (FY25-FY26)" (reported 21-22 Aug 2026)**
- 87.7% of individual traders made net losses in FY26. Before transaction costs, 82.1% were loss-makers. Aggregate net losses fell about 18%, from a *revised* Rs 1,11,788 crore in FY25 to Rs 91,685 crore in FY26. The average net loss per trader rose to about Rs 1.17 lakh. Active individual traders fell 18%, from 106.2 lakh to 87.5 lakh — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rs91685-cr-lost-88-of-individual-traders-lost-money-in-fy26-options-drove-92-of-losses-550474-2026-08-21); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-futures-options-study-transaction-costs-traders-losses-fy26); [Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
- **Note the conflict:** the July 2025 study gave FY25 losses as Rs 1,05,603 crore, while the 2026 study revises FY25 to Rs 1,11,788 crore. Use the revised figure when comparing FY25 with FY26 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html) vs [Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
- Transaction costs for individuals were about Rs 25,000 crore in FY26 (Business Today puts it at Rs 24,800 crore). The average cost per trader rose from Rs 26,027 to Rs 31,628. Costs turned about 4.4 lakh gross-profitable traders into net loss-makers — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rs24800-crore-bill-how-transaction-costs-are-eating-into-retail-derivatives-trading-550694-2026-08-22); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-futures-options-study-transaction-costs-traders-losses-fy26)
- Options made up about 92% of aggregate individual losses. About 97% of traders mainly bought options and about 2% mainly sold them. Option sellers were the only strategy group with a positive *median* return on capital employed in FY26 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rs91685-cr-lost-88-of-individual-traders-lost-money-in-fy26-options-drove-92-of-losses-550474-2026-08-21); [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/business/sebi-fo-loss-study-explained-why-9-in-10-retail-traders-lost-91685-crore-in-fy26)
- Expiry concentration: about 70% of individual index-options turnover happened on expiry day in FY25, falling to about 59% in FY26. In FY26, 75% happened within 1 day of expiry and 97% within 7 days — [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/business/sebi-fo-loss-study-explained-why-9-in-10-retail-traders-lost-91685-crore-in-fy26) (search extract)
- Demographics: 88.5% of traders under 30 lost money, and under-30s were 43% of traders. 73% of traders had incomes below Rs 5 lakh, and that group bore 53% of aggregate losses — [Sarkaritel](https://www.sarkaritel.com/sebi-study-under-30-traders-fno-losses-fy26/); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/89-of-young-fo-traders-lose-money-finds-sebi-study)

**July 2024 intraday cash-segment study (FY19-FY23, released 24 Jul 2024)**
- About 70% (71%) of individual intraday traders in the equity cash segment lost money in FY23. The average loss was Rs 5,371 per trader. The sample was clients of the top-10 brokers, covering about 86% of individual cash-segment clients — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/over-70-intra-day-traders-incur-losses-during-fy23-reveals-sebi-study-124072401110_1.html); [Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-made-losses)
- Trade frequency: loss rates were higher among "very frequent" traders (more than 500 trades a year), and loss-makers on average traded more than profit-makers — [Angel One news](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-highlights-intraday-trading-risk); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html)
- Intraday traders rose from 14.9 lakh in FY19 to 78.3 lakh in FY22, then 68.9 lakh in FY23 (a rise of more than 300%). Under-30s were 48% of intraday traders in FY23. 76% of under-30s lost money, and the under-20 group had the highest share at 81% — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/most-intra-day-traders-incur-losses-half-of-traders-under-30-years-herere-detailed-findings-from-sebi-report); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/over-70-intra-day-traders-incur-losses-during-fy23-reveals-sebi-study-124072401110_1.html)
- A widely reported detail of this study, not re-verified here: among very frequent traders (more than 500 trades a year), about 80% lost money. See Gaps.

### Inferences
- The base rate for an unproven retail intraday strategy is a loss. The FY26 gross-vs-net gap (82.1% vs 87.7% loss-makers) shows that costs alone flip about 5-6 percentage points of the population into loss. A "low-risk" agent therefore has to minimise turnover first and look for edge second.
- Option *selling* had the only positive median outcome, but it carries tail risk and needs much more margin. For a low-risk agent this is a strategy-design trade-off, not a free lunch.
- High trade counts are associated with worse outcomes in the cash segment, so trade frequency should be capped by design.

### Gaps
- I could not access the primary SEBI PDFs. The exact frequency-bucket loss percentages in the 2024 intraday study, and any FY24/FY25 update to the intraday-cash study, could not be verified. I found no evidence of a 2025/2026 follow-up to the intraday cash study.
- I found no figures for the median outcome of retail *algo/API* users specifically.

## 2. SEBI F&O rule changes, Nov 2024 to 2026, and their effect on volumes

### Takeaway
The Oct 1, 2024 circular (six measures, phased in Nov 2024 to Apr 2025) was followed by a May 2025 package. That package fixed expiry days (NSE moved to Tuesday from Sep 1, 2025; BSE stayed on Thursday), introduced delta-based ("FutEq") open interest and new position limits, and added an F&O pre-open session for futures from Dec 8, 2025. Lot sizes were re-based in late 2025: Nifty 65, BankNifty 30, Sensex 20 from Jan 2026. Retail option volumes fell sharply as a result.

### Cited Findings
**Oct 1, 2024 circular ("Measures to strengthen equity index derivatives framework")** — [SEBI circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2024/measures-to-strengthen-equity-index-derivatives-framework-for-increased-investor-protection-and-market-stability_87208.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-announces-six-key-changes-to-curb-speculation-in-derivatives-trading-124100101316_1.html)
- **Contract size:** the minimum value of an index derivative at introduction rose from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh. Lot sizes are set so that contract value on review days stays between Rs 15 lakh and Rs 20 lakh. Effective Nov 20, 2024 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-announces-six-key-changes-to-curb-speculation-in-derivatives-trading-124100101316_1.html); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules)
- **Weekly expiry:** limited to one benchmark index per exchange, so Nifty on NSE and Sensex on BSE. BankNifty, FinNifty and Midcap Nifty weekly contracts ended. Effective Nov 20, 2024 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-announces-six-key-changes-to-curb-speculation-in-derivatives-trading-124100101316_1.html); [Business Standard, Nov 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/new-f-o-rule-to-come-into-effect-from-thursday-nov-21-here-s-what-changes-124112000292_1.html)
- **Expiry-day margin:** an additional 2% ELM applies on short options expiring that day. Effective Nov 20, 2024 — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules); [Arihant](https://blog.arihantcapital.com/sebi-tightens-the-reins-introduces-6-new-measures-for-fo-trading/)
- **Upfront collection of option premium from buyers:** brokers can no longer extend intraday leverage on option buying. Effective Feb 1, 2025 — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules)
- **Calendar-spread margin benefit removed on expiry day:** effective Feb 1, 2025 — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules)
- **Intraday monitoring of index-derivative position limits:** exchanges take at least four random intraday snapshots. Effective Apr 1, 2025 — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules)

**2025 follow-ups ("F&O 2.0")**
- **Expiry days:** a May 26, 2025 circular requires each exchange to choose either Tuesday or Thursday for all its equity-derivative expiries. Only one weekly benchmark-index option is allowed on that day. All other contracts (index futures, non-benchmark index options, stock F&O) must have a minimum one-month tenor and expire in the last week of the month — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-tightens-equity-derivatives-rules-asks-exchanges-to-pick-tuesday-or-thursday/77245.html); [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-fixes-equity-derivatives-expiry-to-either-tuesday-or-thursday)
- NSE moved expiry from Thursday to **Tuesday from Sep 1, 2025**. BSE kept **Thursday** — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/sebi-approves-nses-derivatives-expiry-to-tuesday-bses-to-thursday-lead--20250617171804)
- **Delta-based OI / position limits:** a May 29, 2025 circular moved OI measurement to Future-Equivalent OI (delta-adjusted; for example, 500 long calls at 0.5 delta count as 250 futures). It also revised position limits and added a futures pre-open session. The phased rollout ran from July 1 to December 2025 — [Value Research](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stories/224848/sebi-rewrites-fo-rules-to-curb-expiry-day-speculation/); [Probe42](https://resources.probe42.in/regulatory-updates/sebi-updates/retail-algo-trading-guidelines-2025/)
- Entity-level intraday index-option position limits of roughly Rs 5,000-10,000 crore, effective Oct 1, 2025 (secondary source) — [Tradejini](https://www.tradejini.com/blogs/december-8-futures-trading-changes-nse-bse-guide). These are irrelevant at retail scale.
- **F&O pre-open session** from **Dec 8, 2025**, 9:00-9:15 am. It is a call auction: order entry, modification and cancellation until a random close at 9:07-9:08, then price discovery and matching until 9:12. It covers current-month stock and index futures (and next-month futures in the last 5 trading days before expiry). Options and spreads are excluded — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/nse-to-commence-pre-opening-session-in-f-o-segment-from-december-08-125110400728_1.html); [Jainam](https://www.jainam.in/blog/nse-pre-open-session-fo-segment/)
- **Lot sizes from Jan 2026 expiries:** Nifty 65 (from 75), BankNifty 30 (from 35), FinNifty 60, Midcap Select 120, Nifty Next 50 25, Sensex 20 (from 10). Contracts expiring up to Dec 30, 2025 used the old sizes — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/nifty-lot-size-2026-bank-nifty-sensex); [Arihant Plus](https://www.arihantplus.com/blogs/circulars/nse-revised-lot-sizes-for-major-nifty-index-derivatives-from-october-28-2025)
- **50% cash collateral:** at least 50% of F&O margin must be in cash or cash equivalents, with the rest allowed in pledged securities. From Apr 1, 2026, Zerodha charges Rs 40 per order instead of Rs 20 for intraday F&O when the client's cash shortfall against this ratio exceeds Rs 5 lakh. Zerodha says fewer than 10,000 clients are affected — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/zerodha-raises-fo-charges-to-%E2%82%B940-per-order-for-certain-intraday-trades/); [Equentis](https://www.equentis.com/blog/zerodha-doubles-intraday-fo-brokerage-to-%E2%82%B940-what-it-means-for-traders-from-april-1/)

**Effects on volumes**
- NSE retail options contracts fell from about 397 million in Oct 2024 to 68 million in Feb 2025. Retail options premium fell from a peak of Rs 250 billion (Jun 2024) to Rs 151 billion (Feb 2025) — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebis-fo-regulations-lead-to-drop-in-retail-participation-small-traders-affected-says-report)
- Retail premium turnover and active retail F&O traders fell about 20%. Traders dealing under Rs 10 lakh fell 25%, versus 7% for larger traders — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/retail-option-trading-drops-by-20-percent-after-sebi-measures)
- Index options contracts on NSE and BSE fell from 131.4 billion in FY25 to 62.8 billion in FY26 (about 52%). This came from a search extract; the exact source among the linked articles ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebis-regulatory-changes-have-minimal-impact-on-option-premium-turnover), [Wright Research](https://www.wrightresearch.in/blog/the-real-story-of-zerodhas-volume-crash-and-fandos-new-reality/)) could not be confirmed. Treat it as indicative.
- Outlook Business reported that premium turnover was only modestly affected, in contrast to the sharp drop in contract counts — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebis-regulatory-changes-have-minimal-impact-on-option-premium-turnover)

### Inferences
- Only **Nifty (NSE, Tuesday)** and **Sensex (BSE, Thursday)** have weekly options. BankNifty, FinNifty and Midcap Nifty are monthly only (last Tuesday). Any strategy built on daily-expiry rotation or BankNifty weeklies (common in pre-2024 retail playbooks) is obsolete.
- One Nifty lot is roughly Rs 15-20 lakh notional by SEBI design, so a single futures lot needs about Rs 1.5-2+ lakh margin. Small accounts can practically only buy options (premium paid upfront) or trade equity cash intraday.
- Expiry-day short-option margin is higher (+2% ELM) and calendar-spread offsets are gone, which makes expiry-day option selling more capital-intensive.

- I could not verify primary NSE circular numbers for the Rs 5,000/10,000 crore intraday limits.

## 3. SEBI retail algo trading framework (Feb 4, 2025 circular) and broker-API compliance

### Takeaway
The framework has been fully live since **Apr 1, 2026**, after deferrals from Aug 1, 2025 to Oct 1, 2025 and then a glide path to Apr 2026. Every API order must come from a broker-registered app on a whitelisted **static IP**, through OAuth/2FA sessions that expire daily. Orders are tagged with an algo ID. Above **10 orders per second per exchange per client**, the strategy must be registered with the exchange through the broker. Black-box (logic-hidden) algos sold to others require the provider to be a SEBI-registered Research Analyst. A self-built, self-used, low-frequency agent stays below the registration threshold, but it must run from a static IP and use protected market or limit orders.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI circular "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading", dated Feb 4, 2025. It was originally due Aug 1, 2025, then deferred to Oct 1, 2025 with a phased glide path (milestones to Jan 2026), with full applicability from Apr 1, 2026 — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-extends-deadline-for-retail-algo-trading-framework/); [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/07/29/dcm84-biz-sebi-algo.html); [Tradejini](https://www.tradejini.com/blogs/what-sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-mean-for-you)
- Brokers act as principals and are responsible for all API orders. Algos offered to clients need exchange approval for each algo. Algo providers must be empanelled with exchanges, and retail clients can access approved algos only through registered brokers — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-introduces-new-regulations-for-retail-algo-trading-to-enhance-market-safety); [Tradejini](https://www.tradejini.com/blogs/what-sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-mean-for-you)
- **OPS threshold:** 10 orders per second, per exchange, per client, measured within any calendar second. Below it, no algo registration is needed. Above it, the strategy must be registered through the broker and gets a unique algo ID. Brokers return HTTP 429 above the limit — [Probe42](https://resources.probe42.in/regulatory-updates/sebi-updates/retail-algo-trading-guidelines-2025/); [Fyers support (search extract)](https://support.fyers.in/portal/en/kb/articles/what-are-the-new-sebi-rules-for-retail-algo-trading-from-april-01-2026); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/reyazk08/sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-for-retail-traders-what-changed-in-april-2026-4k04)
- **Algo IDs:** every algo order carries an exchange-assigned identifier, which creates an audit trail — [AlgoBulls](https://algobulls.com/blog/industry-insights-and-updates/sebi-new-algotrading-regulations-for-retail-investors-2026)
- **White box vs black box:** white-box (execution) algos have logic disclosed to the user and can be replicated (VWAP, TWAP, slicers). Black-box algos have hidden logic, and their providers must register as SEBI Research Analysts and keep a research report for each algo — [AlgoBulls](https://algobulls.com/blog/industry-insights-and-updates/sebi-new-algotrading-regulations-for-retail-investors); [Kotak Neo (search extract)](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular)
- **Static IP:** orders are accepted only from a registered App ID mapped to a whitelisted static IP, and others are rejected. Clients may register a primary and a backup IP (one or two) — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-introduces-new-regulations-for-retail-algo-trading-to-enhance-market-safety); [Kotak Neo (search extract)](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular); [QuotaGuard](https://www.quotaguard.com/blog/sebis-static-ip-mandate-is-live-fix-your-cloud-trading-bot-now)
- **Authentication and sessions:** OAuth is the only permitted login method and 2FA is mandatory per session. API sessions are force-closed at the end of each trading day, so a fresh login is needed daily — [Tradejini](https://www.tradejini.com/blogs/what-sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-mean-for-you); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/reyazk08/sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-for-retail-traders-what-changed-in-april-2026-4k04)
- **Order types:** API market orders must carry market protection. Orders with market protection set to 0 are rejected, and this also affects SL-M orders — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/reyazk08/sebis-new-algo-trading-rules-for-retail-traders-what-changed-in-april-2026-4k04); [Kite forum thread title](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15912/preparing-to-comply-with-sebis-retail-algo-rules-static-ip-ratelimits-order-types)
- NSE issued implementation standards in May 2025 (broker-level controls, kill switch, empanelment) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/nse-lays-down-new-ground-rules-for-retail-algo-trading-to-boost-safety-and-compliance-125050600690_1.html); [Zerodha Z-Connect overview](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)
- Zerodha published a dedicated developer notice titled "Preparing to comply with SEBI's retail algo rules (Static IP, ratelimits, order types)" — [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15912/preparing-to-comply-with-sebis-retail-algo-rules-static-ip-ratelimits-order-types); [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/preparing-to-comply-with-sebis-retail-algo-rules-static-ip-ratelimits-order-types/192636); [In the Money by Zerodha](https://inthemoneybyzerodha.substack.com/p/sebi-algo-trading-changes-april-2026)

### Inferences
Practical checklist for a personal, self-coded agent on Kite Connect, Upstox, Dhan, Fyers or Angel SmartAPI:
- Register the app with the broker and whitelist one static IP, plus a backup if offered. A cloud VM with an elastic or static IP, or a static-egress proxy, is needed; home broadband or mobile IPs will be rejected.
- Build a daily OAuth plus 2FA login step. Sessions die at end of day, so plan for TOTP or manual approval each morning.
- Throttle to well under 10 orders per second per exchange. A low-frequency agent never needs exchange registration.
- Use limit orders, or market orders with a non-zero market-protection percentage. Avoid SL-M without protection.
- Self-use of one's own strategy is not "selling a black-box algo", so Research Analyst registration applies only if the strategy or signals are offered to others. This is inferred from the provider-focused wording; it should be confirmed against the circular and the broker's terms.

- I could not fetch the primary circular or the per-broker developer docs. Exact per-broker details (whether a family can share one IP, how often an IP can be changed, whether each broker provides a backup IP slot) were not verified.
- I did not confirm whether the 10 OPS threshold has been revised since Apr 2026.

## 4. Full intraday cost stack and breakeven

### Takeaway
After the Apr 1, 2026 Budget STT hike (futures 0.02% to 0.05% on sell, options 0.1% to 0.15% on sell premium, exercise 0.125% to 0.15%), statutory costs dominate F&O round trips. My estimated round-trip cost (my calculation) is about 0.08% of notional for intraday equity, about 0.06% of notional (about 15 Nifty points) for a Nifty futures lot, and about Rs 1 per unit of premium for a single-lot Nifty option trade, before any slippage.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences (worked breakeven examples; my arithmetic, using the rates above, Zerodha brokerage, NSE)
- **Intraday equity, Rs 1,00,000 buy then sell:**
  - Line items: brokerage Rs 20 + Rs 20, STT Rs 25 (sell side), exchange Rs 5.94, SEBI Rs 0.20, stamp Rs 3 (0.003% on buy), GST about Rs 8.3.
  - Total about **Rs 82, or about 0.082% (8 bps) round trip** before slippage.
  - At Rs 10,000 trade value, brokerage is 0.03% (Rs 3 per side), so the total is about Rs 10.6, or about 0.11%.
  - Breakeven needs a gross move of roughly 0.08-0.11% plus spread and slippage. For a stock with a 0.05% spread, the realistic hurdle is about 0.15-0.2% per round trip.
- **Nifty futures, 1 lot (65 x about 25,000 = about Rs 16.25 lakh notional):**
  - Line items: STT 0.05% on sell = Rs 812, exchange 0.0019% x 2 legs = about Rs 62, brokerage Rs 40, SEBI Rs 3, stamp (0.002% buy, rate not re-verified) about Rs 32, GST about Rs 19.
  - Total about **Rs 970, or about 0.06% of notional, or about 15 Nifty points** per round trip. This compares with about 6-7 points at the pre-April 2026 0.02% STT.
- **Nifty option, 1 lot (65 units at Rs 100 premium, so Rs 6,500 premium each way):**
  - Line items: brokerage Rs 40, STT 0.15% on sell = Rs 9.75, exchange 0.03503% x Rs 13,000 = Rs 4.55, stamp Rs 0.20, GST about Rs 8.
  - Total about **Rs 62.5, or about Rs 0.96 per unit (about 1% of premium)**.
  - With 5 lots, the fixed brokerage spreads out and the cost drops to about Rs 0.38 per unit. Brokerage dominates for small option trades, and STT and exchange fees dominate as trade size grows.
- STT on exercised in-the-money options is charged on intrinsic (settlement) value at 0.15%. That is far larger than STT on premium, so an intraday agent should always square off rather than hold to exercise.

## 5. Taxation of intraday equity and F&O

### Takeaway
Intraday equity profit is **speculative business income**. Speculative losses can be set off only against speculative income and carried forward 4 years. F&O is **non-speculative business income**: losses can be set off against other non-salary business income and carried forward 8 years. Both are taxed at slab rates and need ITR-3. The Income-tax Act 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from Apr 1, 2026 without changing these classifications. Tax audit applies when turnover exceeds Rs 10 crore for fully digital traders, where turnover is the sum of absolute profit and loss per trade.

### Cited Findings
- Intraday equity is speculative under section 43(5) of the 1961 Act. F&O is non-speculative regardless of holding period — [Taxguru/kanakkupillai extract](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/fo-trading-income-tax/); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/income-tax/intraday-trading-tax-audit)
- F&O losses can be set off against non-speculative business income and carried forward 8 years. They cannot be set off against salary, speculative income or capital gains. Speculative losses can be set off only against speculative income and carried forward 4 years — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-f-and-o-trading-in-india-complete-guide-for-fy-2025-26); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/speculative-business-losses-section-73-rules)
- To carry losses forward, ITR-3 must be filed by the due date. A late return forfeits the carry-forward — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-f-and-o-trading-in-india-complete-guide-for-fy-2025-26)
- The Income-tax Act 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from Apr 1, 2026, with no change in the F&O and intraday classifications (sections renumbered) — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-f-and-o-trading-in-india-complete-guide-for-fy-2025-26); [Upstox news](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/income-tax-act-2025-f-and-o-taxation-turnover-calculation-loss-set-off-and-carry-forward-explained/article-200609/); [Patron Accounting section mapping](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/set-off-losses-income-tax-act-2025-section-mapping)
- Tax audit (s.44AB under the old Act): the threshold is effectively Rs 10 crore turnover when cash transactions are at most 5%, which is always true for exchange trading. Turnover is the aggregate of absolute profits and losses per trade, per the ICAI Guidance Note (2022), not contract value. For options, premium on sale is also included — [Taxguru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/fo-turnover-tax-audit-small-filing-error-leads-rs-1-crore-demand.html); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/income-tax/intraday-trading-tax-audit)

### Inferences
- An agent that mixes intraday cash and F&O creates two separate tax baskets. Intraday cash losses cannot offset F&O profits. For tax efficiency, a design that keeps to one segment, or treats intraday cash trades as a separate P&L, is simpler.
- STT is not deductible against income as a tax credit, but it is an allowable business expense when computing business income (standard practice; not separately sourced here).

### Gaps
- New-Act section numbers for audit and presumptive taxation (the old 44AB and 44AD) were not verified.
- Audit triggers when turnover is under Rs 2-3 crore but a loss is declared or profit is below 6% (the presumptive 44AD interplay) were not verified for the 2025 Act.

## 6. Market microstructure: hours, circuits, square-off, margins, liquidity

### Takeaway
NSE and BSE equity trade 9:15-15:30 IST, with an equity pre-open from 9:00 and, since Dec 8, 2025, an F&O futures pre-open from 9:00-9:15. Intraday equity leverage is capped at about 5x (a minimum 20% VaR+ELM margin under peak-margin rules from Sep 2021). MIS positions are auto-squared-off by the broker around 15:20-15:25. Liquidity is concentrated in Nifty weekly options (Tuesday expiry) and Sensex weeklies (Thursday expiry). BankNifty and FinNifty options are monthly only.

### Cited Findings
- Peak-margin regime: from Sep 1, 2021, 100% of upfront margin (VaR+ELM) must be collected and is checked through four random intraday snapshots. Penalties fall on shortfalls. Intraday equity leverage is effectively capped at about 5x, a minimum 20% margin — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/sebi-new-margin-rules-intraday-trading); [Zerodha bulletin, Sep 2021](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/304576/changes-in-intraday-leverages-from-september-2021)
- MIS positions are compulsorily squared off before market close, typically between 15:20 and 15:25 for NSE equity (broker-specific) — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/sebi-new-margin-rules-intraday-trading)
- Price bands: daily bands of 2%, 5%, 10% or 20% for non-F&O stocks. F&O stocks have no fixed band but use a dynamic 10% band (operating range). Market-wide index circuit breakers trigger at 10%, 15% and 20% moves in Nifty or Sensex. A 10% move halts trading 1 hour before 13:00, 30 minutes between 13:00 and 14:30, and not at all after 14:30. A 15% move halts 2 hours before 13:00, 1 hour between 13:00 and 14:00, and for the rest of the day after 14:00. A 20% move halts for the rest of the day — [Upstox help](https://help.upstox.com/support/solutions/articles/41535-what-are-price-bands-and-circuit-breakers-); [Geojit](https://support.geojit.com/support/solutions/articles/89000007110-what-are-price-bands-and-circuit-breakers-)
- F&O pre-open 9:00-9:15 for futures from Dec 8, 2025; options are excluded — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/nse-to-commence-pre-opening-session-in-f-o-segment-from-december-08-125110400728_1.html)
- Weekly options exist only on Nifty (NSE, Tuesday) and Sensex (BSE, Thursday). Lot sizes from Jan 2026: Nifty 65, BankNifty 30, FinNifty 60, Midcap Select 120, Sensex 20 — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/sebi-approves-nses-derivatives-expiry-to-tuesday-bses-to-thursday-lead--20250617171804); [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/nifty-lot-size-2026-bank-nifty-sensex)
- Option buyers pay the full premium upfront (no intraday leverage) since Feb 1, 2025 — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-strengthens-index-derivative-rules)

### Inferences
- An agent must stop opening new MIS positions well before 15:15 and exit itself before the broker's auto square-off. Brokers typically charge about Rs 50 plus GST per auto-squared-off order (not verified in this session).
- The 9:15 open (after the call auction) and expiry afternoons are the highest-volatility windows. A low-risk agent should treat them as no-trade or reduced-size windows unless that is the strategy's specific edge.

### Gaps
- The exact 2026 market trading hours were not re-verified by a fetched source. A proposal to extend F&O hours was discussed in 2024-25, and I found no evidence it was implemented.
- I found no sourced 2026 data on Nifty and Sensex option bid-ask spreads, open-interest depth by strike, or FinNifty/BankNifty monthly liquidity.
- Broker-specific MIS leverage per stock (some brokers offer less than 5x on volatile names) and auto square-off fees were not verified.

## 7. Data and broker API costs and capabilities

### Takeaway
Zerodha Kite Connect order and account APIs have been free since Mar 2025, and the data plan (live websocket plus historical candles, historical free since Feb 2025) now costs about Rs 500 a month. Upstox APIs are reported as free. Dhan charges for data (about Rs 500 a month) with free trading APIs. Fyers is free with REST rate limits. All are subject to the 10 OPS SEBI cap and static IP.

### Cited Findings
- Kite Connect: historical data stopped being charged on Feb 8, 2025 and is included with the base subscription. A free "Personal" API offers execution only, with no market data — [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/49065/)
- Kite Connect data subscription reduced from Rs 2,000 to Rs 500 a month. Order placement and account-management APIs have been free since Mar 2025 — [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/49695/); [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15603/is-order-placement-market-limit-etc-and-gtt-gtt-oco-orders-free)
- Kite Connect rate limits (historical forum posts; may predate the 2026 changes): orders 10 requests per second and 200 orders per minute per API key; daily caps of about 2,000 regular orders (and 2,000 BO/CO); quotes 1 request per second; historical candles 3 requests per second; websocket 3 connections per API key — [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/51470/); [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/21815/)
- Upstox APIs are reported as free (Free and Plus tiers have the same rate limits), and websocket is recommended for high-frequency data. Dhan: trading APIs free, data APIs about Rs 500 a month, and the option-chain endpoint is limited to 1 request per 3 seconds — [Kite forum comparison](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/49609/); [Upstox community](https://community.upstox.com/t/option-chain-rate-limit/12564); [DhanHQ gem docs](https://rubygems.semiweb.ca/gems/DhanHQ/versions/2.7.0)
- Fyers API v3: 200 REST calls per minute and a 10,000-calls-per-day cap (blocked for the day after that). Websocket is not rate-limited in the same way — [Fyers community](https://fyers.in/community/questions-5gz5j8db/post/rate-limit-how-to-find-out-if-my-api-is-reaching-the-rate-limit-threshold-tllsTvel7EHweGE)

### Inferences
- A low-cost stack is a free execution API plus about Rs 500 a month for data (Kite or Dhan), or Upstox free data. Budget for a static-IP VPS on top.
- Websocket ticks are snapshot-style (exchange broadcast feeds are aggregated per broker), not full tick-by-tick, so backtests built on minute candles will overstate fill quality for fast strategies.

### Gaps
- Angel One SmartAPI pricing and limits, Upstox 2026 data-API specifics, the per-websocket instrument caps for each broker (Kite's commonly cited 3,000 instruments per connection), and historical-data depth (how many years of minute data) were not verified with sources in this session.
- I did not verify whether brokers tightened REST rate limits after the Apr 2026 SEBI rollout.
